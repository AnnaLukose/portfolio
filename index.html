About

Curious to a fault, calmest under pressure, and happiest when a system finally does the thing it was refusing to do an hour ago.

I am a final-year Electrical and Computer Engineering student at the University of Cape Town, and I have spent the last few years collecting the kind of problems that do not fit neatly into a lecture.

What connects them is pressure. I work best when there is a clock, a constraint and a real consequence attached to getting it wrong, which is why I keep putting myself in rooms where that is true: the competition floor, a research group, a lab with the students waiting.

I am looking for graduate work where any real-world scenario meets applied machine learning.
